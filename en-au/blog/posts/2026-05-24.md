We are pleased to announce that on the 24th of May 2026, the full season 1 of Warfront, season 1 & 2 of The Battle, and season 3 part 1 of The Battle will be releasing **for free** on the Underworld Collective YouTube channel. We have remastered the audio & editing of the episodes to be combined into 4 videos.

After WatchFlicker shutdown earlier this month, both The Battle & Warfront were left without a place to go (although The Battle seasons 1 & 2 were still available on YouTube). Now, we have remastered and re-released both series in their entirety on YouTube.

That's sort of it, there isn't much to it, [Click Here](https://youtube.com/@UnderworldCollective) to watch.
