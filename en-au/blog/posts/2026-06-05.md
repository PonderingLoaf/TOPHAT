The progress on _StarKry: Sectorfall_ is rapidly well, progressing. We have roughly 4 major additions still being worked on those being UI, the 3d assets, the particle effects (and other post processing effects), and the big one: the server binary. A rough estimate on time to completion from moi (and don't quote me on this) is **about 3 - 4 months** IF, and this is a big if, we continue at the current pace of development. The majority of the game's actual systems are complete and we are currently in the process of implementing said systems with the UI and server since well, it's a multiplayer game.

As promised, here is a new _StarKry: Sectorfall_ logo. It is subject to changes as we continue to develop the brand of StarKry as a whole (not just the game).

![StarKry: Sectorfall Logo](./posts/images/sf_black.png)

We're very surprised that we've been able to make such substantial progress in development of _StarKry: Sectorfall_ in such a short time, only 6 months into actual development and around 80% of the game is complete which is actually crazy, although we're still very much in the early stages of development. Yes seriously, we are early into development even though **~80% of the game is already complete**, we have a very large vision for the game and are excited to see where it goes from here.

I have to thank our wonderful team of artists, developers, and writers who have all helped to actually get the game to the point it is at as of now. They've been working on and off for about 2 years now with this being the third year of us working on what has ultimately become StarKry.
