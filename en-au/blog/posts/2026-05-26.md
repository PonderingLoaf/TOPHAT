As of writing this Underworld is still only a few days old (Only about 24 days old), but we do have some exciting plans for the future. Firstly, *Sectorfall*, we have a substantial number of projects planned for the next 5 years as it is our main franchise. The projects currently planned under the *Sectorfall* banner are:
- Sectorfall
- Sectorfall: Secrets of Morvex
- Sectorfall: Guide to the Galaxy
- Sectorfall: Smugglers

These projects are expected to release at various points throughout the next half decade, the first project in the Sectorfall franchise; "Sectorfall"; will be releasing in early access around September 2026 with a full release in January of 2027. Secrets of Morvex will be releasing around October/November 2026, and Guide to the Galaxy will be releasing around December 2026. And that last project there, we can't explicitly confirm a date yet but what we can say is that we are working on it and there will be more info to come as the project moves further into pre-production and eventually production.

Aside from *Sectorfall* we also have "Warfront" & "The Battle" 2 stop-motion animation series, Warfront officially concluded in 2024 and we **DO NOT** plan on renewing it for future seasons. *The Battle* however was left in limbo with 1 of 4 parts of the final season released after *WatchFlicker* shutdown; earlier this month. The renewal of the series for its remaining 6 episodes is possible and we may revisit it in the future, however we are not actively working on production for it.

*I'll be updating this post soon with more information*
