title: StarKry?
subtitle: A short update on our upcoming projects, and the renaming of our main IP
author: Bodhi Reimer
date: 2026-06-03
---

As many of you know, our primary IP & franchise is *Sectorfall* we've been on and off working on the universe for about 2 years now with the current major project in the franchise being the grand strategy game *Sectorfall*. Through the end of May we began publicising a lot of information including the names of future projects, after some careful consideration the name "Sectorfall" will become a subtitle and we will be introducing a new franchise name "StarKry".

From this point onwards that means the names of our upcoming projects are as follows:
- StarKry: Sectorfall
- StarKry: Secrets of Morvex
- StarKry: Guide to the Galaxy
- StarKry: Smugglers

We feel that the name *StarKry* is substantially better than the previous "Sectorfall" as *Sectorfall* leans to heavily into the grand strategy game idea while we have much larger visions of what StarKry will become. Branding for StarKry: Sectorfall will be released soon with new logos, banners, posters, etc, as we move further into development.

More news about *StarKry: Sectorfall* we have a major amount of development done, we are currently working to finalise our work on combat, fleet management, and multiplayer, following these we will be polishing with new 3d models, better textures, more sound effects, and particles. If all goes according to plan (it won't) we will have the final 3 major features done by September/October with final polish being done through November & December for the game's release in January 2027.
